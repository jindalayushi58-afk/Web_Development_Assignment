Spice Garden Restaurant

Welcome to Spice Garden, a place where traditional Indian flavors meet modern dining. Enjoy freshly prepared dishes made with quality ingredients and authentic spices.

Starters

  • Paneer Tikka - Grilled cottage cheese with Indian spices.
  • Veg Spring Rolls - Crispy rolls filled with fresh vegetables.
  • Chicken Tikka - Marinated chicken grilled with aromatic spices.

Main Course

  • Butter Paneer - Signature Dish - Paneer cooked in a creamy tomato gravy.
  • Veg Biryani - Fragrant rice cooked with vegetables and spices.
  • Chicken Biryani - Traditional rice dish with spiced chicken.

Desserts

  • Gulab Jamun - Soft milk-based dumplings served with sugar syrup.
  • Chocolate Brownie - Warm chocolate brownie served with ice cream.

Beverages

  • Mango Lassi - Refreshing yogurt drink with mango.
  • Masala Tea - Traditional Indian tea with aromatic spices.
  • Fresh Lime Soda - Refreshing lime drink.

Chef's Recommended Dining Experience

  1. Start with Paneer Tikka as an appetizer.
  2. Enjoy our Chef's Special Chicken Biryani.
  3. Finish your meal with Gulab Jamun.
  4. End the experience with a refreshing Mango Lassi.
