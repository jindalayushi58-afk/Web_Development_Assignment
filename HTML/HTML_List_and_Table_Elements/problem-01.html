Admission Overview

GLA University offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs. The admission process is designed to help students apply easily and complete their admission successfully.

Students must fulfill the eligibility criteria before applying.

Eligibility

  • Passed Class 12 or equivalent examination.
  • Meet the minimum percentage required for the course.
  • Have the required subjects for the selected program.
  • Qualify for an entrance examination if required.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official university admission website.
  2. Register with your email and mobile number.
  3. Fill out the online application form.
  4. Upload the required documents.
  5. Pay the application fee.
  6. Check all details carefully.
  7. Submit the application form.
  8. Attend counselling if required.
  9. Complete the admission fee payment.

Application Deadline: 30 June 2026

Required Documents

  • Class 10th Marksheet
  • Class 12th Marksheet
  • Transfer Certificate
  • Migration Certificate
  • Passport-size Photographs
  • Government-issued Identity Proof
  • Entrance Exam Scorecard, if applicable
  • Category Certificate, if applicable

Important Terms

Eligibility
The conditions that a student must satisfy to apply for admission.
Merit List
A list of students selected according to their academic performance or entrance examination results.
Counselling
A process where students receive guidance and select their course.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students.
Registration
The process of creating an account and providing personal details for admission.
