Practical Class Sequence

  1. Read the practical instructions.
  2. Collect the required equipment or software.
  3. Enter the laboratory and take your assigned seat.
  4. Perform the practical according to the instructions.
  5. Record the observations and results.
  6. Submit the practical file to the faculty member.

Academic Rules

  • Students must attend classes regularly.
  • Students must carry their identity cards.
  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during lectures.
  • Students must maintain discipline in classrooms and laboratories.
  • Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.
  • Students must follow laboratory safety instructions.

Important Academic Notice: Internal assessments will be conducted according to the academic calendar.

Important Academic Terms

Lecture
A teaching session where the faculty explains theoretical concepts.
Lab
A practical session where students perform experiments or programming activities.
Tutorial
A session focused on solving problems and understanding concepts in greater detail.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity used to apply theoretical knowledge.
