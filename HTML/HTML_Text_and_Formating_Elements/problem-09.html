Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

All students must carry their valid college ID card while on campus.

Students must follow all instructions issued by the college administration.
Failure to follow official instructions may result in disciplinary action.

Attendance

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject.

Students with attendance below the required percentage may not be allowed to appear in the final examination. Attendance shortage may lead to examination restrictions.

Examination

Students must arrive at the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the exam begins.

Electronic devices, notes, and unauthorized materials are strictly prohibited during examinations.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted through the official submission method specified by the faculty.

Assignment Deadline: Submit all assignments by 5:00 PM on the announced due date.
Late submissions may result in a penalty or reduction in marks.

Discipline

Students must maintain respectful and appropriate behavior with teachers, staff, and fellow students.

Any form of bullying, harassment, or damage to college property is strictly prohibited.
Serious violations may result in disciplinary action according to institutional rules.

Important Notice

Students are responsible for regularly checking official notices and announcements.

These rules are designed to maintain a safe, respectful, and productive learning environment for everyone.
