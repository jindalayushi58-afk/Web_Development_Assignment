Algebra
Square of a Sum(Perfect Square Trinomial) = (a + b)2 = a2 + 2ab + b2

This formula is used to expand the square of two added terms.

Square of a Difference(Perfect Square Trinomial) = (a - b)2 = a2 - 2ab + b2

This formula is used to expand the square of two subtracted terms.

Difference of Squares = a2 - b2 = (a + b)(a - b)

This formula is used to factor the difference of two squares.

Cube of a Sum = (a + b)3 = a3 + 3a2b + 3ab2 + b3

This formula is used to expand the cube of two added terms.

Cube of a Difference = (a - b)3 = a3 - 3a2b + 3ab2 - b3

This formula is used to expand the cube of two subtracted terms.
