Travel Guide: Agra

Attractions

Agra is famous for the beautiful Taj Mahal.

Culture

Agra has a rich Mughal history and traditional art.

Food

Try Agra ka Petha and delicious local chaat.

Best Time

October to March is the recommended season.

Travel Tips

Carry water and book tickets in advance.
